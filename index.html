Autonomous driving research · MATLAB + Simulink

Adaptive driving for unstructured Indian roads.

RoadMind is a research prototype for adaptive path planning and collision avoidance. Its perception-to-control pipeline brings sensing, tracking, prediction, decision-making, planning, and vehicle control together in a MATLAB simulation.
